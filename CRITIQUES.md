Critique 2: Protanopia

Observation: Under protanopia, the different marker shapes help distinguish the three room types. However, many points overlap on the left side of the chart, especially among listings with fewer reviews. This makes individual listings and their room types difficult to identify.

Principle: Overplotting reduces readability because overlapping marks obscure one another. The dense concentration of points also makes it harder to compare listings in that region.

Fix: Expand the lower end of the x-axis using a square-root scale so listings with fewer reviews have more horizontal space. Keep the existing colors, marker shapes, and point-size encoding. Ensure the axis remains clearly labeled.
